How to survive the silence

Explore the island in first person. Walk close to glowing objects and characters, then press E to interact. Find memory shards to unlock the truth. Choices affect the ending; there is no single correct answer.

Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, E to interact, Esc to pause. Click the game after starting to capture the mouse.
Mobile: Use the left virtual stick to move and drag the right side of the screen to look around. Tap the interaction prompt.
